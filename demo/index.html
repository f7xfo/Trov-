🛡️ Why structured, not free text? In Cambodia, publicly accusing an employer in open text can carry legal risk — even when true. Fixed yes/no categories protect both the worker who rates and the integrity of the system. A pattern of "not paid on time" across many workers speaks for itself, safely. This design is why a Ministry can endorse Trov without fear.
