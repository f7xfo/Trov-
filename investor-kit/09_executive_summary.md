## The Problem

Cambodia's recruitment is stuck in 2010. Passive job boards (BongThom, Pelprek) and Facebook groups dominate. There is no AI-powered search, no multi-channel integration, no reputation system for workers or employers.

- **120%+** mobile penetration but no mobile-native job platform
- **2.4M+** Cambodian Telegram users, 0 recruitment bots
- SMEs hire without HR departments; workers have no trusted platform to be found
- Chinese-speaking employers cannot search for Chinese-speaking candidates

## The Solution

**PWA + Telegram Bot + Messenger Bot**, one backend:
- Natural language job matching in **Khmer, English, and Chinese**
- Smart CV intake via AI (PDF, image, text, voice)
- Cross-channel messaging without sharing phone numbers
- Reciprocal rating system — employers rate candidates, candidates rate employers
- Instant alerts when a matching profile appears
